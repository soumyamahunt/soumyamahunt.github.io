Summary

I'm a hands-on Lead Software Engineer with 6+ years of experience building iOS and mobile SDKs for products serving over a billion users. I write production code every day - Swift, Objective-C, SDK design and mobile architecture - while setting technical direction and owning architectural decisions across teams. I lead by building: architecting core systems myself, raising the bar through code review and mentorship, and turning ambiguous business goals into technical roadmaps with measurable outcomes.

Today I own the technical roadmap for MoEngage's mobile SDK suite - iOS, React Native, Flutter and Kotlin Multi-Platform - used by 1200+ global brands. I led the Objective-C to Swift re-architecture of the SDK, its migration to Swift Concurrency with full Swift 6 compliance, and a server-driven UI framework that lets product teams ship personalised experiences to 1B+ devices without an app release.

Before MoEngage I spent three years at TATA Digital, leading a team of eight on the TataNeu superapp - building its networking, state management and server-driven UI foundations, and shipping the grocery and essentials shopping experience end to end. Outside work I maintain open-source Swift libraries and write about Swift, iOS architecture and developer tooling.
