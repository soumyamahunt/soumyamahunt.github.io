Privacy Policy

Stack Smash (also called Lagori) for iPhone and iPad · Effective 4 October 2026

Stack Smash is a party game made by Soumya Ranjan Mahunt ("I", "me"). This policy explains what data the game uses, why, and the choices you have. I don't run any servers for the game and I don't collect your personal data myself; the services described below (Apple and Google) handle data under their own privacy policies.

In short: no account or sign-up. Your bean's name, look and settings stay on your device. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may use device data to show and measure ads, and only personalises them if you allow it. Voice chat is live only and never recorded.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your chosen name, bean skin, settings (such as sound and game speed), best scores, tutorial progress and achievement progress on your device. This data is not sent to me. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads between matches using Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). To serve, measure and protect ads against fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (such as model, operating system and language);
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data about ad performance.

Your choices:

  • Tracking: iOS asks whether the game may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalised using it. You can change this any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and some US states): where the law requires it, a consent form from Google lets you choose how your data is used for ads. You can change your choice any time with the privacy choices (✋) button on the game's main menu.
  • No ads: a Stack Smash Plus subscription removes ads entirely; the ad SDK is then not started.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases (Stack Smash Plus)

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple. I never see your payment details. The game uses Apple's StoreKit to check whether your subscription is active, using the transaction information Apple provides. You can manage or cancel your subscription in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, Apple's service provides online matchmaking, invites, achievements and leaderboards. Your Game Center display name and player ID are shared with the players you play with, and your scores appear on leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy; you can manage it in Settings › Game Center.

Multiplayer and voice chat

  • Nearby play connects to devices close by over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth (iOS asks for Local Network access). Your bean's name and look are shared with players in your lobby. Game data goes directly between the devices in the match.
  • Voice chat is available in nearby and online matches and is off until you turn on the microphone (iOS asks for microphone access). Your voice is streamed live to the other players in the match, through the host's device, and is never recorded, stored, or sent to me. You can mute your mic or all voices at any time during a match.

Analytics and tracking

The game does not include any analytics or crash-reporting service of its own. Apart from AdMob as described above, it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

Stack Smash is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. I don't knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the game, contact me and I'll help remove it where I can.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or privacy laws in some US states), you may have rights to access, delete, or object to the use of your personal data. Because I don't hold your data myself, most requests are best made through the controls above or directly with Apple and Google. If you need help, contact me and I'll point you to the right place.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data use changes, I'll update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the App Store release notes.
