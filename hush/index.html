Hush

Block ads and trackers in Safari and every app, for iPhone and iPad.

Hush blocks ads, trackers and known malware, with lists built from the ones uBlock Origin uses by default. It costs $2.99 on the App Store and works two ways, and you can use either or both:

  • Safari (included): Safari extensions that block ads and trackers and hide the empty boxes ads leave behind.
  • Hush Everywhere (optional subscription): blocks in every app using iOS's built-in URL filtering, so it works alongside any VPN, including a work one.

Your browsing stays private in both. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what happens to your data.

Frequently asked questions

How do I turn on Safari blocking?

Open the Protection tab and tap the switch for each Safari blocker, which takes you to its page in Settings. You can also go to Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions and turn on Hush Ads, Hush Privacy and Hush Cosmetic. Once a blocker is on, its switch in Hush pauses and resumes it.

Which mode should I use?

If you only browse in Safari, the Safari blockers are enough. To block ads in other apps too, add Hush Everywhere. It works alongside any VPN you already use, including one from work.

Why does iOS ask to add a filter?

Hush Everywhere is a network setting that only you can allow. iOS asks the first time you switch it on, and blocking starts once you allow it. You'll find it under Settings › General › VPN, DNS & Device Management, though it isn't a VPN and doesn't route your traffic.

What happened to VPN mode?

Apple only allows VPN apps from developers registered as a company, so Hush 1.0 ships without VPN mode. Hush Everywhere blocks in every app instead.

How do I block a site that isn't on the list?

In the Blocklist tab, add a host such as ads.example.com, a full web address, or paste a whole hosts file. Hosts you add block straight away in Safari. Hush Everywhere gets its answer from its filtering service, so there a host blocks once the service's list includes it too; Check status shows which do.

A page or app broke. What can I do?

In Safari, pause the blocker that's causing it from the Protection tab. For Hush Everywhere, turning off Settings › Block when unsure lets requests through when the filtering service can't be reached. If a site on the list shouldn't be, email me and I'll look at it.

Why do I still see ads in YouTube videos?

Video ads in YouTube come from the same addresses as the video itself, so no URL or DNS blocker can stop them without stopping playback too. Hush blocks the parts that are separate, such as banner slots and ad measurement.

How do I manage or cancel Hush Everywhere?

In Hush, go to Settings › Manage subscription, or use Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your device. To use it on a new device, tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account. Safari blocking comes with the app and keeps working either way.
