Hush

Block ads and trackers in Safari and every app, for iPhone and iPad.

Hush blocks ads, trackers and known malware, with lists built from the ones uBlock Origin uses by default. It works three ways, and you can use any of them:

  • Safari (free): Safari extensions that block ads and trackers and hide the empty boxes ads leave behind.
  • VPN mode (free): blocks ads and trackers in every app by answering their lookups on your device. It can also connect through your own WireGuard VPN provider.
  • Hush Everywhere (subscription): blocks in every app using iOS's built-in URL filtering, so it works alongside any VPN, including a work one.

Your browsing stays private in all three. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what happens to your data.

Need help? Email support@soumyamahunt.com. Please include your device model, iOS version, Hush version (shown in Settings) and which mode you use if something isn't working.

Frequently asked questions

How do I turn on Safari blocking?

Open the Protection tab and tap the switch for each Safari blocker, which takes you to its page in Settings. You can also go to Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions and turn on Hush Ads, Hush Privacy and Hush Cosmetic. Once a blocker is on, its switch in Hush pauses and resumes it.

Which mode should I use?

If you only browse in Safari, the Safari blockers are enough. To block ads in other apps too, use VPN mode, unless you already use a VPN you can't replace (for example one from work). iOS runs one VPN at a time, so in that case Hush Everywhere is the one that works alongside it.

Can I use VPN mode with my own VPN?

Yes, if your provider offers WireGuard configuration files (Mullvad, Proton VPN, IVPN, AirVPN and Windscribe do, for example). Import the file in the VPN tab and Hush connects to your provider, blocking ads and trackers before they enter the tunnel. With a provider you can also refuse direct connections to addresses that were never looked up, which catches apps that skip DNS, though it can break some calls and games.

Why does iOS ask to add a VPN or a filter?

VPN mode and Hush Everywhere are network settings that only you can allow. iOS asks the first time you switch either on, and blocking starts once you allow it.

How do I block a site that isn't on the list?

In the Blocklist tab, add a host such as ads.example.com, a full web address, or paste a whole hosts file. Hosts you add block straight away in Safari and VPN mode. Hush Everywhere gets its answer from its filtering service, so there a host blocks once the service's list includes it too; Check status shows which do.

A page or app broke. What can I do?

In Safari, pause the blocker that's causing it from the Protection tab. For Hush Everywhere, turning off Settings › Block when unsure lets requests through when the filtering service can't be reached. If a site on the list shouldn't be, email me and I'll look at it.

Why do I still see ads in YouTube videos?

Video ads in YouTube come from the same addresses as the video itself, so no URL or DNS blocker can stop them without stopping playback too. Hush blocks the parts that are separate, such as banner slots and ad measurement.

How do I manage or cancel Hush Everywhere?

In Hush, go to Settings › Manage subscription, or use Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your device. To use it on a new device, tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account. Safari blocking and VPN mode stay free either way.
