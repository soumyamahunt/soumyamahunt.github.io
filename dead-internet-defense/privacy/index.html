Privacy Policy

Dead Internet Defense for iPhone and iPad · Effective 18 October 2026

Dead Internet Defense is a game made by Soumya Ranjan Mahunt ("I", "me"). This policy explains what data the game uses, why, and the choices you have. I don't run any servers for the game. The services described below (Apple and Google) handle data under their own privacy policies; the only data I can see is what you put in the Arena, described below.

In short: no account or sign-up. Your progress, settings and the forum you design stay on your device and in your own iCloud. If you play the Arena against other players, your forum, its defence, your nickname, rating and raids are stored in the game's iCloud database where other players can see them. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may use device data to show and measure ads, and only personalises them if you allow it.

Data stored on your device and in your iCloud

The game saves your progress (stars per raid, your best Endless wave, lifetime counts for achievements), the forum you design (thread and subforum names, entry links, rules and theme) and your settings (sound, haptics and tutorial hints). If you're signed in to iCloud, the same save is kept in your iCloud key-value storage so it follows you to your other devices. That copy is stored by Apple in your own iCloud account under the Apple Privacy Policy. None of this is sent to me. The member names and posts you see in the game are made up and built into the app.

The Arena (playing against other players)

The Arena lets you raid other players' forums and lets them raid yours. Until you clear the first act of the campaign it's practice against forums the game makes up, and nothing leaves your device. After that, when you open the Arena while signed in to iCloud, the game stores the following in its public CloudKit database, which Apple runs under the Apple Privacy Policy:

  • an identifier CloudKit gives you for this game (it isn't your Apple Account, name or email);
  • your Game Center nickname, if you're signed in to Game Center, otherwise "Moderator" and a few characters of that identifier;
  • your forum's design (thread and subforum names, links and rules), your Arena defence and AI moderator style, your forum level and your Arena rating;
  • each raid you make: the forum you raided, your bot deck, when you sent each squad, and the result, so the other player can watch it; and
  • any report you make about another forum, with the reason you picked.

Other players can see your nickname, forum, defence and rating, and replays of raids on their forums. Names that players type are checked against a list of offensive words before anyone else sees them, and you can hide other players' names entirely in Settings > Arena. You can block any player and report any forum from the Arena. I can read this data to look into reports. Your Arena rating may also be posted to a Game Center leaderboard.

To have your Arena data removed, email support@soumyamahunt.com from the Game Center nickname or forum name you used, and I'll delete it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads using Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK): a banner on the campaign screen, an occasional full-screen ad between raids, and a reward ad for extra starting karma only when you choose one. To serve, measure and protect ads against fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (such as model, operating system and language);
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data about ad performance.

Your choices:

  • Tracking: iOS asks whether the game may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalised using it. You can change this any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and some US states): where the law requires it, a consent form from Google lets you choose how your data is used for ads. You can change your choice any time in the game's Settings › Ad privacy choices.
  • Fewer ads: the one-time Supporter Pack purchase turns off the banner and between-raid ads. The reward ad is then never shown; the karma boost becomes free.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases (Supporter Pack)

The purchase is sold and billed by Apple. I never see your payment details. The game uses Apple's StoreKit to check whether you've bought it, using the transaction information Apple provides.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, your Endless best, the number of deepfakes you've caught and your achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy; you can manage it in Settings › Game Center.

Analytics and tracking

The game does not include any analytics or crash-reporting service of its own. Apart from AdMob as described above, it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

Dead Internet Defense is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. I don't knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the game, contact me and I'll help remove it where I can.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or privacy laws in some US states), you may have rights to access, delete, or object to the use of your personal data. Because I don't hold your data myself, most requests are best made through the controls above or directly with Apple and Google. If you need help, contact me and I'll point you to the right place.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data use changes, I'll update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the App Store release notes.
