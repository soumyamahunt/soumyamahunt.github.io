Dead Internet Defense

A 3D strategy game where you keep the last human forum human, for iPhone and iPad.

The internet is mostly bots now. You moderate HumanCorner, one of the last forums where real people still talk. Steer the bots by locking and merging threads, place defences that slow your own members too, and spot the deepfakes hiding among them. Design your own forum, then defend it through 18 campaign raids and an Endless mode, and raid other players' forums in the Arena.

Frequently asked questions

How do I place a defence?

Pick Mod, CAPTCHA or Rate limit in the bottom bar. The empty slots beside each thread light up; tap one. Tap a placed defence to remove it for half its karma back.

What do Lock, Merge and Split do?

Lock closes a thread so bots take another route, but its members get bored. Merge joins a thread into its neighbour in the same subforum, so their bots, members and CAPTCHA gates end up in one place. Split undoes a merge.

How do I tell a deepfake from a real member?

Tap a member to open their profile. Deepfakes repeat themselves and never reply to anyone, and later ones steal a real member's profile photo. In early raids they also post far too often, and their names and join dates give them away. Tap Watch to keep an eye on a suspect first. Banning a real member costs trust and upsets their thread, so check before you ban.

How does the Arena work?

Open Arena on the title screen. Build a bot deck, find a forum and raid it: pick a squad at the bottom and tap an entry tab to send it in. The other player isn't online; their own defences and AI moderator fight back. Under Your defence, set up how your forum defends itself when others raid you, and watch those raids in the Defence log. Until you clear the first act of the campaign, the Arena is practice against made-up forums.

Someone's forum has an offensive name. What can I do?

Tap Report or Block on their forum or in your defence log, and you won't be matched with them again. You can also hide every player-made name in Settings > Arena.

How do I get more threads, subforums and rules?

Earn stars in campaign raids. Stars raise your forum level, which unlocks more threads, subforums, entry links and rule slots in Design your forum.

Does my progress sync between devices?

Yes, if you're signed in to iCloud. Your stars, forum design and settings sync through your own iCloud account.

How do I restore the Supporter Pack on a new device?

Open Settings (or Support the game on the title screen) and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Why do I see ads?

Ads keep the game free. A banner only appears on the campaign screen and full-screen ads only between raids, never during one. The karma-boost ad only plays when you choose it. The one-time Supporter Pack turns off ads, makes the boost free and adds three themes. Where the law requires it, you can change your ad privacy choices in Settings › Ad privacy choices.
